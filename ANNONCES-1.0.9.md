### English version

**Title:** LMU Stats Viewer 1.0.9 — now in Italian, richer community leaderboards

> Hi everyone,
>
> **LMU Stats Viewer 1.0.9** is out. Quick reminder: it's a free Windows app that reads Le Mans
> Ultimate's result files to give you your stats, records, telemetry, a voice race engineer and an
> AI coach — all local, no account needed.
>
> **🇮🇹 Italian, 5th language**
> The app is now fully available in Italian: interface, race engineer voice callouts (two Italian
> neural voices, Paola and Riccardo, to download from Config → Audio / Voice), offline voice
> commands, and the AI coach, which answers in Italian.
>
> **🏆 Community leaderboards**
> - Every community leaderboard is listed, with the record and its holder, even on tracks you've
>   never driven.
> - The "My combos" button highlights yours.
> - Click a track to open its all-classes leaderboard; click a class to filter it.
> - A leaderboard's detail matches the website: your position, the full standings (sectors,
>   version, date) and the lap-time spread.
> - Your flag (the country from your in-game profile) and your Steam avatar appear next to your
>   name — never in anonymous mode, and the avatar can be removed in one click.
>
> **🏎️ Multiclass races**
> Your position in your class now comes first (Live page, overlays, live AI coach), with the
> overall position next to it.
>
> **🔁 Filters kept**
> Each page keeps its filters, sort order and view when you leave and come back (reset when the app
> restarts).
>
> **🐛 Fixes**
> - AI coach: brake bias was read the wrong way round (inverted advice).
> - ohne_speed level: track variants (Bahrain Paddock, Sebring School, Le Mans Mulsanne…) were
>   compared to the main layout.
> - Telemetry: flat traces on some laps, wrong S2/S3 sector times.
> - Sessions page opened from a link: the car/layout filters were lost.
>
> **Update**: offered automatically when you start the app.
> **New install**: https://github.com/cparfait/lmustatsviewer/releases/latest
> **Website**: https://lmustatsviewer.cparfait.ovh/ — **Online leaderboards**: https://lmu.cparfait.ovh
>
> Thanks to everyone for the feedback — it shaped a good part of this release. See you on track!
